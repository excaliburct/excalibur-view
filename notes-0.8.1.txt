A SHEET'S SCALE IS KEPT BY THE OFFICE

A scale set on a sheet of an office drawing (Measure, Set Scale..., a scale
picked from the list, a calibration, or one put on every sheet) now goes to
the office server with the markups. It is there the next time the drawing
opens, on every computer, and the office server measures the takeoff with it.
Before, it lived only in the copy on the computer it was set on, and was gone
the next time the drawing opened.

A SCALE CLEARED ON ONE COMPUTER IS CLEARED ON EVERY ONE

Taking a sheet's scale off reaches every computer that has the drawing open,
and the drawing's own scale, if it names one, applies again. A scale the
office server won't keep (it isn't a real scale) is said once, and the
markups keep syncing.

A TAKEOFF PULLED BY FABWIRE IS MEASURED AS THE VIEWER MEASURES IT

Each measurement is worked out with the scale it was drawn with, the one the
viewer writes on every measurement, and the sheet's own scale only for a
markup that carries none. Measurements drawn on a calibrated sheet no longer
come back to Fabwire as "their sheet has no scale". A markup measured at a
different scale than its sheet, or carrying a scale that can't be measured
with, is marked so Fabwire says it. A scale of nothing, below nothing, or
absurdly large is never measured with, and one whose words say another scale
than its numbers is refused.

FOR PROGRAMS

POST /api/v1/sets/{id}/scale sets or clears a sheet's scale; GET
/api/v1/sets/{id}/sheets says it as "measure". See docs/integrating.md.
