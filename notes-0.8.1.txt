YOUR SCALE STAYS PUT

A scale you set on a sheet is now kept by the office server, so it is still
there the next time you open the drawing, and every computer measures with
it. Before, it was kept only on the computer that set it.

TAKEOFFS COME THROUGH WHOLE

Measurements sent to Fabwire use the same scale you see on screen, so a
takeoff no longer comes back with its lengths left out.

SCALES ARE CHECKED

A scale that can't be right is refused, and a measurement taken at a
different scale than its sheet is flagged so it gets a second look.
