A SIMPLE SCREEN FOR THE SHOP AND THE FIELD

Shop and field accounts on an iPad or Android tablet now get a simple
layout: a list of their jobs, then big buttons for Drawings, Shipping and
Documents. No folder tree, no clutter. The office keeps the full layout on
its computers.

ONLY RELEASED DRAWINGS

The shop and field see released sheets only. A new revision shows a NEW
badge, and sheets that were replaced or never released stay out of sight.

SHIPPING SIGNED ON THE TABLET

Shipping tickets are signed right on the tablet. Received By, Loaded By and
Driver each show only for the people allowed to sign them.

THE OFFICE DECIDES WHAT EACH TABLET SHOWS

From Fabwire, the office picks what the shop and the field see on each job,
pins documents like a site plan or bolt form, and names the crew. A job
shows on a tablet once it has a crew.

FIXES

A computer turned away by an office that takes secure connections only now
always sees why. Small fixes to how the office server starts up.
