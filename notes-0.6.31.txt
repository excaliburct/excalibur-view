PASTING ONTO A SHEET

Ctrl+V and Edit, Paste now put down whatever was copied last, from anywhere.
Words copied in an email, a spreadsheet or a web page go on the sheet as a
text box. A screenshot or a photo goes on as a picture. Markups copied in
Excalibur View still paste as markups, the same as before.

It works on every sheet, blank sheets added to a set included. Ctrl+V puts
it where the mouse is; the menu puts it in the middle of what you're looking
at. A picture file dragged onto a sheet goes where it is let go.

Before this, Ctrl+V did nothing on Windows, the Mac or Linux, even for
markups copied here. Edit, Paste worked only for markups.

TEXT BOXES

The words in a text box now break their lines in the same places while you
type as they do once the box is finished, so nothing jumps around when you
click away.

Two spaces stay two spaces, an indent stays an indent, and the line breaks in
a text box made in Revu are kept, both when it is opened here and when its
size or font is changed. Before, a Revu note could come back as one long
line. A note of several lines is also saved with its line breaks in the
rich-text copy of its words that Revu reads.

Accents, degree signs, plus-or-minus and fractions are kept exactly as typed
through every save. Emoji are kept in the words, though the standard PDF
fonts show them as a question mark on the sheet.
