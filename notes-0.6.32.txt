TEXT BOXES ON TURNED SHEETS

A text box or callout put on a sheet that is stored sideways in the PDF now
reads upright, the same way the drawing does. Before, its words came out
turned a quarter, running up the sheet, and there was no way to turn them
back. Sheets combined from shop drawings are often stored this way.

The box also grows the right way as you type: it keeps the width you drew
and gets taller down the sheet, instead of running sideways.

A box already saved sideways by an earlier version comes upright the next
time you move it or change its words, size or look. Text boxes on ordinary
sheets are saved exactly as before.
