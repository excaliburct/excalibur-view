# Edit Text: what it does, what was wrong, where it goes next

Dated 2026-11-25. Branch `view-pdf-edit-20261125`.

## What it does today

Edit Text changes the words already printed on a sheet (not markups). It
works at the level of pdfium's page objects:

1. **Reading** (`render.rs`, `Engine::pieces`): every text object on the
   sheet, and now inside Form XObjects too, becomes a `Run`: its words
   (pdfium's text page), its start and direction (the text matrix with the
   CTM, then any form matrices), its size (Tf times the matrix's vertical
   scale) and how far it runs (pdfium's tight, turned box for the object).
   Invisible text (OCR layers, render mode 3) and text a later picture lies
   wholly over (OCR set under a scan) are left out.
2. **Grouping** (`textedit.rs`, `paragraphs_ruled`): runs are grouped by
   direction, then into lines (one baseline, close gaps, no ruled line
   between), then into paragraphs (one size, one font and weight, one
   spacing, lined up, no ruled line between, a new numbered note starts a
   new one).
3. **Showing**: the paragraph under the pointer is outlined. With **Text
   Outlines** on (View menu, the Advanced Text toolbar, `Ctrl+Shift+B`),
   every paragraph is outlined faintly. Off by default, kept in each person's
   settings file. Words inside a form are outlined grey.
4. **Editing**: a click opens an editor over the paragraph, with the
   sheet's own line breaks (drawing notes are broken by hand; only a line
   that grows past the paragraph's width wraps). Its letters are set at the
   sheet's size and zoom, spaced so the widest line runs as far as the
   sheet's (within 2 px: egui sets letters on its own grid), with the sheet's
   line spacing, and its first baseline and first letter on the sheet's
   (within 1 px). Text that is turned on the screen
   (or smaller than 7 screen points) is edited in a box just below it instead.
5. **Writing** (`rewrite_in`): the old objects are taken out, new ones set
   with the same direction and size, the page regenerated by pdfium, saved
   incrementally, and refused unless the rest of the sheet draws exactly as
   before. Words inside a form are refused: a form can be on every sheet.

## What was wrong (found 2026-11-25)

Measured on hand-made fixtures (`crates/hyperview/tests/edit_text_alignment.rs`;
shots in `~/build/view-pdfedit-shots/`):

- **A box round every paragraph at once.** On a real sheet that is hundreds of
  blue boxes over the drawing. Now only under the pointer unless asked.
- **Tables came up one box per column.** On the repo's sample set (Northgate,
  sheet S-001) the sheet index and abbreviations schedules each read as two
  "paragraphs": the whole SHEET column, header included, and the whole TITLE
  column. Clicking one title opened the column and would have reflowed it as
  one paragraph. Now no line or paragraph crosses a ruled line drawn on the
  sheet (cell borders from `re` or line segments, inside forms too), and a
  change of font or weight starts a new paragraph (a bold heading over its
  notes).
- **The editor came up off its words**: a different font size clamp, egui's own
  wrap and margins, the paragraph run together and re-wrapped at a width, placed
  at the box's square corner, horizontal over turned text. Now fitted and
  placed as above, and tested.
- **Words inside Form XObjects were invisible to the tool** (flattened Bluebeam
  markups and stamps, sheets drawn as one form). Now found, outlined grey, and
  refused with a sentence saying what to do instead.
- **Turned text ran long**: its length came off the square box round the
  turned box (a third too long at 45 degrees). Now the turned box.
- **OCR words under a scan** (visible text with the scan drawn over it) were
  offered for editing over a picture. Now left out.

The matrices themselves (Tm with CTM, `cm` scaling, `Tz`, `Tc`, CID/Type0
with ligatures and TJ kerning, Type3, /Rotate 0/90/180/270, cropped sheets)
were already right: every box sits on its ink within 1.5 px at 2 px/pt.

## What remains, and the recommended engine

1. **Font fidelity (biggest).** Nearly every real drawing embeds *subset*
   fonts. Typing a letter the subset lacks switches the whole paragraph to
   Helvetica, Times or Courier. Fix: when the subset's family is installed
   (Arial, Romans/simplex TTF, Calibri...), load the whole system font with
   `FPDFText_LoadFont` (already in pdfium-render) and set the paragraph in it.
   No new crate: font folders are read with `std::fs`. About 300 lines and a
   day, with tests on an embedded subset fixture.
2. **Character-level grouping.** pdfium objects are one per show operator, so
   a TJ holding two title-block cells is one piece. Grouping from pdfium's
   per-character API (`FPDFText_GetCharBox`, `GetCharOrigin`, `GetMatrix`) would
   split by real gaps. Editing would still remove whole objects, so a split
   object needs both halves rewritten. About 2 days.
3. **Editing inside a form** when the form is used once in the whole file
   (`FPDFFormObj_RemoveObject`, then regenerate). Needs a prototype to confirm
   pdfium 7881 regenerates form streams. About 2 days.
4. **Clipped text** is outlined where the clip hides it. Clip paths are
   readable (`FPDFPageObj_GetClipPath`); about half a day.

Not recommended now: an own content-stream editor (byte-range replacement of
Tj/TJ operators plus a font subsetter). It is how Acrobat-grade editing
works and would keep the rest of the stream byte-identical, but it needs a
subsetting crate that is not in Cargo.lock (for example `subsetter`, MIT or
Apache-2.0) and is two to four weeks. pdfium's reading is proven right by
the tests here, so the work is better spent on 1 to 3.
