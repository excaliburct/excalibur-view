SIGNING TRAVELERS AND SHIPPING TICKETS ON THE SHOP'S IPADS

An office program can now ask for sign-offs on a drawing set: a traveler's
stations (B12 · WELD, B12 · FINAL INSPECTION) or a shipping ticket's
receiver. When the drawing is open in Excalibur View, a button over it says
how many are waiting. It opens the list beside the drawing, grouped as the
program grouped them. Tap Sign on a line, sign with a finger, a pen or the
mouse, check your name, and tap Sign. The program reads the signature back
and fills in its own form.

Who can sign is who can see the drawing: its job, and its folder's rule. The
shop's viewers sign the shop's papers; the office's papers stay the office's.

A signature is final. Once signed, a line can't be signed again or taken
back, and the server keeps who signed (their account and the name they wrote),
when, and the signature itself. Only a person signs: a program's key can ask
for sign-offs and read them back, never sign. Every signature is on the audit
log.
