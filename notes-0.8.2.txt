NO MORE BOXES ALL OVER THE SHEET

Edit Text no longer draws a box around every note on the sheet. Only the
words under your pointer are outlined. To see every box, turn on Text
Outlines, next to Edit Text or on the View menu, or press Ctrl+Shift+B. It
starts off, and your choice is remembered.

TABLES AND HEADINGS STAY APART

A sheet index or schedule is read one cell at a time, not one whole column.
A bold heading is no longer lumped in with the notes under it.

THE EDIT BOX SITS ON THE WORDS

The box you type in opens right on top of the words, the same size and
spacing, with the lines broken where the drawing breaks them. Words turned
sideways or at an angle open in a box just below them.

STAMPS ARE FOUND BUT PROTECTED

Words inside a flattened stamp or markup now show, outlined in grey. They
can't be changed in place, because the same stamp can be on every sheet,
and View says what to do instead.
